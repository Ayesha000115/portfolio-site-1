COLLABORATION ROADMAP

My Working Process

A clear, dependable five-stage journey designed to take the stress out of building a website, keeping you informed at every step without confusing technical jargon.

Stage 1

Discovery

Understand your business, what services you offer, your target customers, and what you want the website to achieve.

Stage 2

Planning

Agree on the exact pages, features, visual direction, content requirements, and project timeline before any building begins.

Stage 3

Design & Build

Build and customise the website structure, responsive styling, typography, and interactive contact points according to the plan.

Stage 4

Review & Test

Test responsiveness across mobile, tablet, and desktop, check all links, and verify WhatsApp booking buttons.

Stage 5

Launch & Handover

Deploy the approved website to hosting, connect your custom domain name, and provide you with all agreed files and guidance.

PREPARATION GUIDE

What Do You Need to Get Started?

Don't worry if your materials aren't 100% finished. We can start with whatever you have and refine as we progress.

01. Business Overview

A brief description of your business, what products or services you provide, and your location or service area.

02. Photos & Logo

Your logo file (if available) and photos of your work, salon, or workspace. Clean smartphone photos work great!

03. WhatsApp Number

The phone number you want customers to contact when booking treatments, consultations, or asking questions.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Every project is priced fairly based on the scope: a focused landing page or small business starter site is significantly more affordable than a comprehensive multi-service website with treatment galleries and WhatsApp booking flows.

I provide an upfront, fixed quotation before any work begins, so there are never hidden costs or unexpected charges. Payments are typically split into milestones (a project deposit to commence planning, and the remaining balance upon final approval prior to live launch).

Most business websites and salon sites take between 1 to 3 weeks from initial discovery to final deployment, depending on the number of pages and how quickly content (photos, treatment menus, service details) is provided.

I build lightweight, modern static websites that deploy seamlessly to high-speed global static hosting platforms like Netlify. Netlify provides lightning-fast loading speeds, 99.99% uptime, and an automatic free SSL certificate.

I will personally guide you through purchasing or connecting your custom domain (e.g., yourbusiness.com) so everything is live, secure, and running smoothly.
