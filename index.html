QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, transparent answers about pricing, turnaround timelines, and the web development process.

Every project is priced fairly based on the scope: a focused landing page or small business starter site is significantly more affordable than a comprehensive multi-service website with treatment galleries and WhatsApp booking flows.

I provide an upfront, fixed quotation before any work begins, so there are never hidden costs or unexpected charges. Payments are typically split into milestones (a project deposit to commence planning, and the remaining balance upon final approval prior to live launch).

Most business websites and salon sites take between 1 to 3 weeks from initial discovery to final deployment, depending on the number of pages and how quickly content (photos, treatment menus, service details) is provided.

If you have an urgent deadline or an upcoming grand opening, let me know during our initial WhatsApp chat—fast-tracked projects can often be accommodated.

Don't worry if you don't have everything fully written out! To start, all you need is a basic idea of what services you offer, your logo (if you have one), and any reference websites or photos you like.

I help guide the structure, outline the necessary sections, and assist with clear wording and high-resolution layout aesthetics during our Planning stage.

Instead of forcing potential customers to register accounts, remember passwords, or fill out complicated forms, your website includes direct WhatsApp buttons tailored to each service.

When a customer taps "Book on WhatsApp", it opens WhatsApp on their phone or desktop with a prefilled message (e.g., "Hi, I'd like to book an appointment for Hair Styling on Saturday"). You receive the booking directly in your chat and can confirm instantly.

I build lightweight, modern static websites that deploy seamlessly to high-speed global static hosting platforms like Netlify. Netlify provides lightning-fast loading speeds, 99.99% uptime, and an automatic free SSL certificate (the secure padlock icon).

I will personally guide you through purchasing or connecting your custom domain (e.g., yourbusiness.com) so everything is live, secure, and running smoothly.

Yes, absolutely. The code is written in clean, well-commented HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with zero bloated frameworks. Updating a price, telephone number, or photo is straightforward, and I am available for ongoing updates or maintenance whenever your business evolves.

STEP BY STEP

My Working Process

A transparent, stress-free path from your initial idea to a published website ready to welcome visitors.

Stage 1

Discovery

Understand your business, what services you offer, your target customers, and what you want the website to achieve.

Stage 2

Planning

Agree on the exact pages, features, visual direction, content requirements, and project timeline before building begins.

Stage 3

Design & Build

Build and customise the website structure, responsive styling, typography, and interactive contact points according to the plan.

Stage 4

Review & Test

Test responsiveness across mobile, tablet, and desktop, check all links, and verify WhatsApp booking buttons.

Stage 5

Launch & Handover

Deploy the approved website to Netlify hosting, connect your custom domain name, and provide all files and guidance.
